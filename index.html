Who we are

Small team, high standards,
production software.

Corveth is a philanthropy ministry: we believe using AI well is a way to serve humankind, so we hold it to a higher bar before it ships. Decisions get written down and reviewed before implementation starts, and claims about performance or security ship with evidence you can check yourself — not just our word. The goal is software that genuinely improves the lives of ordinary people.

Decisions on the record

Every material technical choice is written down — what was weighed and why — before implementation starts. No black boxes.

Proof you can re-run

Performance and correctness claims ship with reproducible evidence, not marketing numbers. Verify it — don't take our word for it.

Adversarial by design

Security posture is pressure-tested against a hostile threat model throughout the build, not hardened after the fact.
